Acme Retail Ltd is a fictional company created for demonstration. In fiscal 2025 Acme reported revenue of 4,820 crore rupees, up 12 percent from 4,304 crore in fiscal 2024. Growth was driven mainly by online sales, which rose 31 percent and now account for 38 percent of total revenue. Operating margin improved to 11.4 percent from 10.1 percent as logistics costs fell after the company opened three new regional warehouses.

Net profit for fiscal 2025 was 412 crore rupees compared with 331 crore the previous year. Total debt declined to 1,150 crore rupees from 1,420 crore, and the debt to equity ratio dropped to 0.42. The board proposed a dividend of 6 rupees per share.

Risk factors include rising competition from quick commerce platforms, dependence on a small number of suppliers who provide 45 percent of inventory, and currency exposure on imported goods. Management warned that a slowdown in urban consumer spending could reduce store footfall, which still generates 62 percent of revenue. The company plans capital expenditure of 600 crore rupees in fiscal 2026 to expand warehouses and upgrade its mobile app.
