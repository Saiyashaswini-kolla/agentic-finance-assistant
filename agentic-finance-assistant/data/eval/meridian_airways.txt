Meridian Airways Ltd is a fictional airline created for demonstration. In fiscal 2025 Meridian carried 21 million passengers, up 17 percent from 18 million in fiscal 2024. Revenue rose to 7,400 crore rupees. The fleet stands at 74 aircraft, and an order for 20 more was placed during the year. Fuel is the largest cost, at 38 percent of total expenses.

The company narrowed its net loss to 260 crore rupees from 610 crore the previous year. Total debt is 5,400 crore rupees, and management aims to cut it by a fifth over three years.

Risks include volatile fuel prices, delays in engine deliveries from suppliers, and currency losses because many lease payments are made in dollars. Management expects to break even in fiscal 2026.
