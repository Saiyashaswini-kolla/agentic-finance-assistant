Borealis Bank Ltd is a fictional bank created for demonstration. In fiscal 2025 Borealis reported net interest income of 9,200 crore rupees, up 8 percent from 8,519 crore in fiscal 2024. Deposits grew 14 percent to 118,000 crore rupees, helped by a push into salary accounts. Gross non-performing assets fell to 2.1 percent of loans from 2.9 percent as recoveries improved.

Net profit was 2,340 crore rupees compared with 1,980 crore the previous year. The capital adequacy ratio stood at 16.5 percent, well above the regulatory minimum. The board proposed a dividend of 9 rupees per share.

Risks include rising delinquencies in unsecured personal loans, exposure to cyber attacks on digital channels, and margin pressure if interest rates are cut. Management said branch expansion will continue, with 250 new branches planned in fiscal 2026 at a cost of 700 crore rupees.
