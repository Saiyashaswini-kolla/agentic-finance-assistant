Cygnus Pharma Ltd is a fictional drug maker created for demonstration. In fiscal 2025 Cygnus reported revenue of 3,150 crore rupees, up 9 percent from 2,890 crore in fiscal 2024. Export sales account for 58 percent of revenue, led by generic medicines sold in North America and Europe. Research spending was 480 crore rupees, about 15 percent of revenue, and the company won two new drug approvals during the year.

Net profit for fiscal 2025 was 520 crore rupees compared with 447 crore the previous year. The company has no long term borrowings and holds 900 crore rupees in cash. The board proposed a dividend of 4 rupees per share.

Risk factors include price cuts on generic medicines in the United States, dependence on a single plant that produces 40 percent of total output, and the chance of a failed regulatory inspection. Management plans to open a second plant in fiscal 2027.
